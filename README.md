## The short version

**Austin Carranza** — Frontend Developer & Software Engineering student at **Universidad Técnica Nacional** 🇨🇷

I build digital products at the intersection of **design, music, and code**. My flagship project, [**Teclia Academia**](https://github.com/YAustinXYZ/Teclia-Academy), is a piano learning platform I designed and shipped from scratch — not a tutorial clone, a real product with auth, interactive lessons, and an Apple-inspired UI.

> *"Great interfaces don't announce themselves. Users just feel that everything is in the right place."*
